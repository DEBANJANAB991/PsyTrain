# Hackathon Project: Meet Your AI Patient

**OTH Amberg-Weiden** – Reinvent medical history-taking with AI and Apple Vision Pro (macOS required)

## Challenge Description

Talking to patients is one of the most important skills in medicine and one of the hardest to learn from a textbook. This challenge is about building an AI-powered virtual patient for Apple Vision Pro that allows students to practice medical history-taking in a realistic and immersive environment.

Imagine entering a room, meeting a patient and starting a conversation. The patient has symptoms, a medical history, emotions and a personality and responds dynamically to what you ask and how you communicate. Your goal is to create a prototype that combines generative AI, virtual patients and spatial computing to make this interaction as realistic and educational as possible.

But the conversation is only half the challenge: after the consultation, the system should help the student learn from it:

- Did they ask the right questions?
- Did they miss important information?
- Was the interview well structured?
- How did they communicate with the patient?

Think beyond a simple chatbot. Explore how AI and immersive technology can create a new kind of training environment where students can practice, receive feedback, improve, and try again.

**Your mission:** Build the next generation of medical history-taking training.

Find more information regarding this challenge in the interview with challenge owner Prof. Dr. Eva Rothgang, Program Director, Medical Engineering.
